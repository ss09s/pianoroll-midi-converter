Source encodings: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/

This app bundles a subset of the source recordings. Each note was converted
to mono PCM, trimmed and faded. Bass recordings received presence EQ and levels
were adjusted per sample. The recordings are packed into piano-samples.wav.
